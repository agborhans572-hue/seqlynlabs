Ignite X Capsules are a multi-compound research formulation containing GLP1S, BPC-157, and 5-Amino-1MQ. This encapsulated formulation is intended exclusively for laboratory investigations involving GLP-1 receptor activity, peptide characterization, NNMT inhibition, formulation stability, and associated cellular signaling pathways.
GLP1S is a modified peptide analog of glucagon-like peptide-1 and has been characterized as a GLP-1 receptor agonist. Laboratory applications may include receptor-binding analysis, peptide stability testing, molecular characterization, and investigations of downstream GLP-1-associated signaling.[1]
BPC-157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide investigated primarily through preclinical laboratory and animal models. Research involving BPC-157 has examined peptide stability, molecular interactions, and several cellular signaling processes. Human evidence remains limited, and available findings do not establish safety or effectiveness for this combined formulation.[2]
5-Amino-1MQ, also known as 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, is a synthetic small-molecule compound studied as an inhibitor of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase, commonly abbreviated as NNMT. Published research has examined its activity in biochemical assays, cultured cells, and preclinical models involving nicotinamide-associated metabolic pathways.[3]
Research involving the combined formulation should be conducted using appropriately designed laboratory protocols.
